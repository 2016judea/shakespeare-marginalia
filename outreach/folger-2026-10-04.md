Subject: King Lear with Melville, Milton and Keats in the margins — built on your text

I built a reading edition of King Lear that carries the actual marginalia of three readers: Melville's 52 marks from Melville's Marginalia Online, Milton's from the Free Library's First Folio, Keats's from Spurgeon. Each mark sits beside the line that provoked it, on your modern text. 124 pages, free, non-commercial, credited to the Folger Shakespeare on the title page.

https://aidanjude.vercel.app/king-lear-annotated

The pipeline is open source and not Lear-specific. Point it at any play and a reader's marks, declared as the text of the line rather than a line number, and it renders the edition. Melville's marks match automatically from the MMO XML; anyone else's go in a small JSON file.

https://github.com/2016judea/shakespeare-marginalia
